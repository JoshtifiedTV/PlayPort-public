Hosting together. On your terms.

Game servers and websites, managed from one portable executable. No install, no runtime to ship, no external database.

Minecraft servers

Vanilla and Paper, with automatic downloads and updates from Mojang and PaperMC.

Steam games

Boots SteamCMD for you — installs and updates dedicated servers with live progress and update timers.

Website hosting

Static, PHP/FastCGI and reverse-proxy sites, each on its own port with its own file manager.

Automatic HTTPS

One-click Let's Encrypt with a built-in ACME client, self-signed certs for LAN use, or your own PFX.

Cloudflare tunnels

A temporary public URL with no account and no open ports, or named tunnels for stable hostnames.

One folder, portable

Everything lives in a playport-data/ folder beside the exe. Delete it to uninstall completely.
