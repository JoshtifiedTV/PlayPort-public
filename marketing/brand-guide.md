# PlayPort brand guide

## Positioning
PlayPort is an all-in-one self-hosting platform. Lead with ownership, simplicity and the freedom to host your way. Game servers and websites are examples, not the limits of the brand.

**Official slogan / primary headline:** Play your way  
**Supporting line:** Hosting together. On your terms.  
**Descriptor:** All-in-one self-hosting platform.  
**Default CTA:** Meet PlayPort.

## Visual identity
The pixel-edged controller contains a server rack: gaming and hosting in one mark. Lime identifies the left half; violet identifies the right.

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lime | `#a2ef00` | Primary accent, headlines, CTA background |
| Violet | `#872bff` | Secondary accent and logo |
| Midnight | `#10101c` | Campaign background, CTA text |
| Deep purple | `#22103a` | Background gradient |
| Near white | `#f5f5fa` | Headings and body text on dark |
| Muted lavender | `#c8bfdc` | Supporting copy on dark |

Use Arial/Helvetica for editable campaign artwork and system sans-serif for web presentation. Headlines are bold, short and sentence case. Small labels may be uppercase. These system fonts need no external download; verify line wrapping when exporting on a different machine.

## Logo rules
- Use the full-color transparent logo on dark, quiet surfaces.
- Use the black mark on light surfaces and white mark on dark photography.
- All three marks have transparent negative space. Do not add a black rectangle behind a logo when a transparent asset is requested.
- Leave clear space of at least one server-bay height around the mark.
- Keep proportions intact. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor or add shadows to the mark.
- The browser icon uses the same mark on a square transparent canvas. At very small sizes it is a brand cue rather than a detailed illustration.
- Campaign banners and avatars intentionally include a background; they are not transparent logo assets.

## Voice
Direct, capable and welcoming. Talk to people self-hosting personal projects and communities, not only game servers. Prefer “Play your way” to “Revolutionary infrastructure orchestration.”

Avoid unverified claims: instant setup, guaranteed uptime, unlimited resources, supported games, integrations, price, testimonials or benchmark numbers. Add specific claims only after release verification.

## Accessibility
Use near-white or lime text on midnight. Violet is mainly decorative: avoid small violet text on dark backgrounds. Never rely on color alone to explain status. Supply meaningful alt text, such as “PlayPort: a lime and violet game controller with a server rack at its center.”
