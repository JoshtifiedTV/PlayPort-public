# PlayPort launch plan

A checklist and schedule for taking PlayPort public. Replace the bracketed
fields (`[PRODUCT_URL]`, `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`, dates) before publishing, and treat
the schedule as a template rather than a fixed calendar.

## Before you announce (pre-launch)

- [ ] Decide the download page URL and set `[PRODUCT_URL]` everywhere in
      [`copy.md`](copy.md).
- [ ] Choose the support address and set `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`.
- [ ] Confirm the release build is the one you are shipping
      (`download/PlayPort.exe` and its `.sha256`).
- [ ] Post the download and its [disclaimer](../DISCLAIMER.md) together - the
      unsigned-build warning should be visible before anyone runs the exe.
- [ ] Pick the campaign mode: **Explore** (live product) or **Follow**
      (pre-launch). Do not imply availability that does not exist yet.
- [ ] Prepare the primary artwork from the kit (square, portrait, story, link
      card) so every channel uses the same set.

## Launch day

- [ ] Publish the site (`index.html`) and confirm every link resolves.
- [ ] Announce on the channels you chose, using the launch announcement copy.
- [ ] Pin or pin-equivalent the announcement where the community gathers.
- [ ] Watch the download for the first few hours; answer questions in the same
      voice as the brand guide.

## After launch (first two weeks)

- [ ] Reply to feedback and collect the most common questions - they are the
      first candidates for the manual and the FAQ.
- [ ] Re-check the artwork crops on each platform.
- [ ] Note what to change for the next release and feed it back into
      [`copy.md`](copy.md) and this plan.
